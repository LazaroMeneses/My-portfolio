About Me

I'm a front-end developer focused on creating modern, responsive, and search-engine-friendly websites and web applications. I work with React.js, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and UI/UX best practices to turn ideas into fast and polished digital experiences.

What motivates me most about programming is understanding the language of the future. I'm fascinated by how, through code, we can communicate with computers and bring ideas to life. What began as curiosity has become a long-term passion for building useful products.

My current goal is to grow as a freelancer and collaborate on real-world projects that challenge me, improve user experience, and deliver measurable results. In the long term, I aspire to build my own web products with strong performance, accessibility, and SEO foundations.
